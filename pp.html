FAMR Privacy Policy

INTRODUCTION

FAMR is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

  • Account Information:Name, email, phone number, and payment details (for paid plans).
  • Form Data:Crop details, expenses, reports, and farm-related activities.
  • Usage Data:How you interact with the platform, including log files and cookies.

    • HOW TO USE YOUR DATA

  • To provide and improve FAMR services.
  • To personalize user experience and offer relevant insights.
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions.
  • To enhance security and prevent fraud.

    • DATA SHARING & SECURITY

  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties.
  • Data is stored securely with encryption and access controls.
  • We may share anonymized data for research and industry analysis.

    • COOKIES & TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES

  • FAMR uses cookies to improve functionality and track user engagement.
  • Users can adjust cookie preferences in their browser settings.

    • THIRD-PARTY INTEGRATION

  • FAMR may integrate with third-party services (e.g., payment processors, weather APIs).
  • These services have their own privacy policies, which users should review.

    • USER RIGHTS

  • You can access, update, or delete your data at any time.
  • You can opt out of marketing communications.

    • CHANGE TO PRIVACY POLICY

  • We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Users will be notified of significant changes.
